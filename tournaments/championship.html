Overview
WSGA Men's Club Championship
Friday, August 28, 2026 · 9:00am Shotgun

The premier individual competition of the Men's Club season — 18 holes on the North/South course with both gross and net championships on the line. You must be a Men's Club member playing to a WSGA handicap.

Entry Fee

$20.00 — includes dinner. Does not include green fee or cart fee.

Sign up as an individual or a 2-some — 4-somes will be broken up.

★ Limited to the first 100 players — call early to reserve your spot.

Call the Pro Shop to Sign Up →
Nemadji Pro Shop · 715-394-0266
Tournament Schedule
Sign-Up — Until the Field Fills

Call the Nemadji Pro Shop at 715-394-0266 to enter. The field is capped at 100 players and fills on a first come, first served basis.

Friday, August 28 — Championship Round

9:00am shotgun · 18 holes on the North/South course. Dinner follows golf and is included with your entry fee.

Tournament Rules
Format

18-hole individual stroke play, one 9:00am shotgun start on the North/South course. Both a gross and a net championship are contested, plus skins and proximity contests.

Eligibility

Must be a Men's Club member playing to a WSGA handicap. Field limited to the first 100 players.

Tees (By Age)

59 and under: Black/White tees · 60 to 69: White tees · 70 and older: White/Green combo.

Pairings & Prizes

You may sign up as an individual or as a 2-some; 4-somes will be broken up. A player may win either net or gross — not both.

Prize List

A player may win either net or gross — not both. Skins and proximity contests are paid separately.
