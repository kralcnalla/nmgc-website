Tournament Rules
Saturday Format — Best Ball

Each member of each team plays their own ball and holes out on every hole. After applying each player's respective handicap, the team score is the best 2 scores on the par 5's and the best 3 scores on the par 3's and par 4's.

Sunday Format — Las Vegas Scramble

Each team member hits each shot. The team decides which is the best shot and plays the succeeding shot from that point. If the selected shot is in the fairway, players must play within one club length of the original ball. If the selected shot is in the rough or a bunker, that player's shot must be hit first as the ball lies; the other players must drop within one club length of that point and in the same cut. In a bunker, the ball may be placed by the remaining players. Playing order is by mutual agreement except as stated above.

Tees (Both Days)

A & B players: Black tees · C & D players: White tees · E players over 65 years old: Green tees (mandatory), under 65 years old: White tees.

Handicap Rules / Qualifying Handicaps

All entries are reviewed by the handicap committee, determined by:

  1. Current computer (USGA/GHIN) index, or handicap card with index.
  2. Entry for players without a USGA/GHIN index, or with an index more than 20.0, will be allowed at the discretion of the handicap committee prior to the team drawing.
  3. All handicaps and tee assignments are subject to review and adjustment at the discretion of the handicap committee prior to the team drawing.

If you have no GHIN, use 70% of your best league handicap (18 maximum).

Additional Rules

All tee times will be strictly maintained. Failure of a player or team to make their tee time will force that team to use the rules for absent or late players as outlined by the rules committee. No putts may be conceded — everything must be putted out (no gimmies). Winter rules in your own fairway in all events. All disputes are decided by the Tournament / Rules / Handicap Committee: Al Kurtz, Rick Lear & Russ Drobot.
